WHY limit_hover_card.py IS ZERO
-------------------------------
It emits no literals of its own. Its public surface is
  set_html(self, html: str, ignore_filters: bool = False) -> None
It renders HTML that limit_gauges.py has already built and translated, so it
imports no tr(). The board's verify clause says all five files must "import
fastprompter.core.translations.tr"; for this one that clause is unreachable
without inventing a literal that does not exist. Recorded rather than
papered over.
