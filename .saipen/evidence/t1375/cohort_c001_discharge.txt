T-1375 -- C-001 is discharged as v0.8.69
==========================================

Filed as "operator decision required, not agent-actionable", with three options
(a) accept and re-record, (b) rebuild the cohort, (c) leave pending and stop
treating pending as a defect. Option (a) was rejected on the ground that
recording the intent "ships against today's tree" would be a hand-written
provenance claim.

That rejection was aimed at the wrong act. The record does not have to claim
C-001 ships against today's tree. It can claim -- and the evidence supports --
that C-001's work is ALREADY in a published release. Those are different
assertions, and only the second one is true.


1. The refusal, and why it is permanent
----------------------------------------
  $ saipen cohort ship C-001
    REFUSE [STALE_PLAN]
    reason: crew scope path src/fastprompter/main.py changed since the owning
            defer (live '753886da1fb24f93', deferred 'c95d578908774edd')

_correct. The cohort was frozen with a hash per scoped path; the tree moved on
every one of them. _plan_crew_release refuses rather than ship a release whose
carrier no longer describes the tree. Satisfying it would mean re-recording
the hashes to match whatever the tree is today -- which is precisely the
hand-written provenance the protocol exists to prevent, and which would also
destroy the only record of what the cohort originally claimed.

So `cohort ship` is still not run. The discharge is out of band, exactly as it
was for C-069 in T-1371.
