THE BLOCKER

  $ saipen cohort ship C-001
    REFUSE [STALE_PLAN]
    reason: crew scope path src/fastprompter/main.py changed since the owning
            defer (live '753886da1fb24f93', deferred 'c95d578908774edd')

The cohort was frozen with a hash per scoped path. The live tree has moved on
every one of them since. _plan_crew_release (release.py) recomputes live hashes
and refuses rather than ship a release whose carrier no longer describes the
tree -- the refusal is correct and must not be worked around by re-recording
hashes to match whatever the tree happens to be today.

WHY THERE IS NO WAY FORWARD FROM INSIDE THIS REPO

The entire cohort surface is two verbs:

    cohort status <C-###>
    cohort ship  <C-###>

There is no re-freeze, no rebind, no refresh, no amend. Once a cohort's recorded
hashes disagree with the tree, it is permanently un-publishable through the
engine. Confirming the surface:

    $ saipen ticket --help | tr ',' '\n' | grep cohort
    cohort status <C-###>
    cohort ship <C-###>

This is the same dead end T-1371 found for C-069, and it is worse than "stale":
for C-069, 4 of 6 recorded hashes correspond to no blob that exists in any ref
of this repository, so that cohort cannot be re-derived even in principle --
those bytes were never committed. For C-001 the hashes are merely stale, so a
human who accepts "ship C-001 against today's tree" could record that intent --
but only the engine may write the record, and the engine offers no verb for it.

WHAT AN OPERATOR HAS TO DECIDE

  (a) ACCEPT AND RE-RECORD: declare C-001 (and C-069) published against the
      current tree. Requires a new engine verb to re-freeze, or a hand edit of
      .saipen/kitchen/cohort_registry.json -- which the release path may reject
      as an unprovenanced carrier, and which would be exactly the kind of
      hand-written provenance this protocol exists to prevent.
  (b) REBUILD: close C-001/C-069 as never-published and re-run the cohort with
      fresh member hashes from the current tree. Loses the historical link
      between the cohort and the work it closed.
  (c) LEAVE PENDING: accept that some closed cohorts were never published,
      record that as a known terminal state, and stop treating
      publication_status: pending as a defect for them.

None of these is agent-actionable: each decides what the release ledger asserts
about the past, which is the operator's call, not a cleanup.

VERIFY CLAUSE

  .saipen/kitchen/cohort_registry.json shows both C-001 and C-069
  terminal-published, OR T-1371 is re-scoped to cover both.

Neither arm is reachable here. The ticket is BLOCKED, not DONE.
