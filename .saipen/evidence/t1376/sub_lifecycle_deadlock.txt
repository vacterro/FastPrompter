T-1376 -- the deadlock was real; the missing lever was a STATUS, not a verb
==========================================================================
SUPERSEDED 2026-10-01. This file previously concluded "There is no in-repo
verb that opens the cycle" (line 29, retained below verbatim). That
conclusion was WRONG. The resolution is in `sub_drain_resolution.txt`.

THE ERROR, RECORDED SO IT IS NOT REPEATED
-----------------------------------------
Every verb tried returned a refusal, and five refusals in a row were read as
proof the cycle was unopenable. They were proof about the five verbs tried.
The engine encodes the whole disposition policy in one place and that place
was never read:

  saipen_engine/sub_clean.py, OUTBOX status gate:
      allowed = {"ready", "draft", "blocked", "reviewed", "stale"}
      only ready / draft / blocked block a clean
  saipen_engine/subs.py:139
      OUTBOX_STATUSES = ("ready", "draft", "blocked", "reviewed", "stale")

`stale` is the engine's own sanctioned marker for "this package's freshness
keys have moved; it is not collectable and must not block cleanup" -- which is
exactly the condition this ticket describes. Its absence from the tried-verb
set is the whole reason the ticket looked blocked.

A refusal is evidence about the verb that refused, not about the verb space.
Before declaring a protocol cycle unopenable, read the policy that encodes the
decision (the enum, the gate table, the status set), rather than enumerating
verbs until the answers stop coming.
