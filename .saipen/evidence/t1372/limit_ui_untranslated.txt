5) WHY THE 100% COVERAGE GATE NEVER SAW IT (the part that matters for the fix).
   tools/i18n_utils.py:collect_tr_keys walks the tree and collects keys ONLY from
   call nodes whose func is `tr`, `tr_fmt`, or `<expr>.tr` / `<expr>.tr_fmt`
   (lines 29-53). A user-facing literal that was never wrapped in tr() contributes
   NO key, so it is invisible to coverage by construction.

   fails on a user-facing literal in a module with no tr import.
